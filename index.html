Mars

The Red Planet

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and one of the most fascinating world in our Solar System.

With its red deserts, enormus volcanous, ancient river valleys, and two small moons, Mars has fascinated humans for centuries.

But Mars isn't just an intresting planet - it may also hold clues about wether life ever existed beyond Earth.

Mars at a glance

Distance from the Sun? About 228 million km
Length of a Day? 24 hours and 39 minutes
Length of a Year? 687 Earth days
Moons? 2 — Phobos and Deimos
Average Temperature? About -60°C
Atmosphere Mostly carbon dioxide
Gravity? About 38% of Earth's
Planet Type? Rocky planet

Water on Mars

Mars looks likke a dry, dusty desert today, but scientists have found strong evidence that water once flowed across its surface.

Billions of years ago, Mars appears to have had rivers, lakes, and possibly larger bodies of water

scientists have discoverd acient river channels, lakebeds, and minerals that formed in the presence of water.

Where is the Water Now?

Most of Mars's remaining water is frozen

Large amounts of ice exist beneath the surface of the polar region.

Scientists are also investigating whether water could exist deep underground

Did Mars once look like Earth?

Acient Mars was probably very different from the cold, dry planet we see today

Its atmosphere was once thicker, and its surface had environment where liquid water could remain for long periods.

Could these ancient watery environments have supported life?

Could There Have Been Life on Mars?

Scientists have not found confirmed evidence of life on Mars.

However, Mars may once have had some of the conditions needed for life as we know it.

On Earth, life exists in environments containing liquid water, so ancient Martian rivers and lakes are especially interesting to scientists.

The most likely type of life scientists are searching for is acient microbial life - tiny orgainisms rather than intelligent or complex creatures

Searching for Signs of Ancient Life

NASA's Perseverance rover landed on Mars in 2021

It is exploring Jezero Crater, a location that once contained an ancient lake and river delta

Perseverance is studying Martian rocks and collecting samples that could potentially contain evidence about Mars's ancient environment

Scientists are particullary intrested in rocks that may have preserved chemical or physical traces of ancient microbial activity

The Big Question

Did Mars once have environments capable of supporting life?

Evidence suggests that ancient Mars had at least some environmentthat could have been habitable

Did life actually exist there?

That remains unknown...

Amazing Mars Facts

Why Is Mars Red?

Mars gets its reddish color from iron minerals in its rocks and dust that have oxidized — basically, rusted.

Home of a Giant Volcano

Mars has Olympus Mons, the largest known volcano in the Solar System. It rises about 22 km above the surrounding surface.

A Day Is Similar to Earth's

One Martian day, called a sol, lasts about 24 hours and 39 minutes.

Mars Has Two Moons

Mars is orbited by Phobos and Deimos, two small, irregularly shaped moons.

A Helicopter Flew on Mars

NASA's Ingenuity became the first aircraft to make a powered, controlled flight on another planet.

Mars Has Dust Devils

Huge spinning columns of dust can sweep across the Martian surface. Some can reach hundreds of meters in height.

Blue Sunsets

Unlike Earth's sunsets, which often appear red or orange, sunsets on Mars can appear bluish near the Sun.

Exploring Mars

Humans have never walked on Mars.

So far, our exploration has been carried out by robotic spacecraft, landers, rovers, and orbiters.

These machines help scientists learn about:

  • 🪨 Martian rocks and minerals
  • 💧 The history of water
  • 🌡️ Mars's climate and atmosphere
  • 🧬 The possibility of ancient life
  • 🏠 Whether humans could someday explore Mars

The Mystery of Mars

Mars is more than just a red dot in the night sky.

It may contain clues about how planets change, how water disappears, and whether life could have existed beyond Earth.

The biggest mystery remains:

Did life ever exist on Mars?

We don't know yet.

But as spacecraft continue exploring the planet and studying its ancient rocks, scientists are getting closer to finding the answer.
